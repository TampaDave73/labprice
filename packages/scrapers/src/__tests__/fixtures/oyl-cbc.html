A Complete Blood Count (CBC) with Differential and Platelet count is a comprehensive blood test that provides essential information about the cellular components of blood. This test measures red blood cells (RBCs), white blood cells (WBCs), platelets, hemoglobin, hematocrit, and various red blood cell indices. The differential portion counts the different types of white blood cells (neutrophils, lymphocytes, monocytes, eosinophils, and basophils) as percentages and absolute numbers. This test is fundamental for evaluating overall health, detecting various blood disorders, monitoring treatment responses, and screening for infections, anemia, leukemia, and other hematologic conditions. The CBC is one of the most commonly ordered laboratory tests and serves as a cornerstone for medical diagnosis and monitoring. Abnormal results can indicate a wide range of conditions from simple nutritional deficiencies to serious blood cancers.
Markers Included
Hematocrit; hemoglobin; mean corpuscular volume (MCV); mean corpuscular hemoglobin (MCH); mean corpuscular hemoglobin concentration (MCHC); red cell distribution width (RDW); percentage and absolute differential counts; platelet count (PLT); red cell count (RBC); white blood cell count (WBC)
