Who Needs a Comp Metabolic Panel (14) Test?

Patients requiring a broad evaluation of their overall health, or those presenting with unexplained fatigue, jaundice, or changes in urination, should undergo a Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP 14). This extensive diagnostic assay is critical for identifying metabolic syndrome, chronic kidney disease, and hepatobiliary pathology. Routine CMP monitoring provides a fundamental snapshot of systemic homeostasis, guiding timely therapeutic interventions for acute and chronic organ dysfunction.

Why test?

The Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP 14) evaluates fundamental systemic homeostasis by analyzing hepatic function, renal excretory capacity, electrolyte balance, and glycemic status. This broad diagnostic assay measures 14 critical biomarkers, including liver transaminases, blood urea nitrogen, creatinine, and fasting glucose, to detect acute and chronic organ dysfunction. Routine CMP monitoring is essential for identifying metabolic syndrome, chronic kidney disease, and hepatobiliary pathology, guiding timely therapeutic interventions.

Know more about DrSays Lab service

  • How to request an itemized receipt for a lab order?

    If you purchased a lab order directly, we can provide an itemized receipt with your order information once the order is completed. To request an itemized receipt:

    • Log in to your DrSays account.
    • Go to the Labs tab.
    • Click on Receipt under the specific lab order.
    • Select Request itemized receipt.

    Please note: We are unable to issue an itemized receipt that includes physician information and diagnostic codes, as this requires a doctor’s appointment.

  • Where do I go for my lab testing? (¿A dónde debo ir para mis análisis de laboratorio?)

    Please note that DrSays is a virtual medical clinic. All lab testing purchased through DrSays must be completed at a Labcorp location. Please do not visit the DrSays office for lab testing services.
    (Tenga en cuenta que DrSays es una clínica médica virtual. Todos los análisis de laboratorio adquiridos a través de DrSays deben realizarse en una ubicación de Labcorp. Por favor, no visite la oficina de DrSays para servicios de análisis de laboratorio.)

  • Can I use the same lab slip more than once?

    No. Every lab slip is valid for a one-time collection only. Reusing a lab slip is prohibited unless expressly authorized by DrSays. Doing so without authorization may lead to processing delays, an inability to support your testing, or additional charges.

    To remove a test: Please contact us at support@drsays.org or send us a support message for assistance. 

    To add a new test: Please place a new order for the additional test. Be sure to bring both lab slips to your lab appointment. 

  • My doctor has ordered lab tests for me, what do I do next?

    Please note that Doctor-Ordered (MD) lab orders are available for 7 days only. If the order expires and you still wish to complete the tests, please contact us at support@drsays.org or send us a support message.

  • What is a reflex test? 

    A reflex test is a follow-up test performed on the same sample only when the results of an initial screening test fall outside the normal range.

    Not all lab tests include reflex testing, and additional testing will only be performed if specific reflex criteria are met. Additional fees and processing time may apply for reflex or confirmatory testing.

    Benefit from ordering tests with reflex through DrSays

    • Cost savings: Reflex testing is bundled into a single price, which can be more cost-effective than ordering follow-up tests separately.
    • Time savings: You do not need to place a new order or return to a lab if reflex criteria are met. Additional testing is performed automatically using the original specimen.
    • Convenience: No need for additional lab visits or separate payments if reflex testing is triggered.
    • Transparent pricing: Reflex testing prices are included upfront and reflect comprehensive, all-inclusive pricing.

    How it works

    • Initial screening: A standard lab test is performed first.
    • Reflex trigger: If results are abnormal or meet predefined criteria, the lab automatically performs additional reflex testing on the same sample.
    • Follow-up clarification: The reflex test provides more detailed information to help confirm or clarify the initial findings, such as supporting a diagnosis or identifying the cause of an abnormal result.
  • What is the difference between a Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Order and Doctor-Ordered (MD) Labs?

    Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Order

    • Tests are ordered directly by the user through DrSays; no appointment is required.
    • Payment is made through DrSays (out-of-pocket).
    • If you wish to discuss or review your results with a doctor, you will need to book a separate consultation appointment.
    • Lab testing is provided exclusively through LabCorp and cannot be completed at other laboratory companies. Please use the LabCorp location finder to find a nearby LabCorp testing center and complete your tests.

    Doctor-Ordered (MD) Labs

    • Tests are ordered by a licensed doctor after a clinical consultation.
    • Payment may be made through DrSays, insurance, or self-pay, depending on eligibility.
    • Results are reviewed and interpreted by the ordering provider before being released to the patient.
    • Please note that further treatment or prescriptions are not guaranteed.
    • You may use your lab requisition to complete testing at any laboratory testing center that accepts the order.
