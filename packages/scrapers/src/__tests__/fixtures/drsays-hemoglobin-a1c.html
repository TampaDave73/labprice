Who Needs a Hemoglobin A1c Test?

Patients experiencing excessive thirst, frequent urination, or unexplained fatigue should undergo a Hemoglobin A1c test to evaluate long-term blood glucose levels. This metabolic metric is indispensable for individuals at risk of diabetes mellitus or those needing to monitor the efficacy of their glycemic control protocols. Routine tracking facilitates targeted adjustments to treatment regimens, mitigating the risk of cardiovascular and microvascular complications.

We offer a wide range of blood tests to meet your health needs. No insurance is required. You may order lab tests directly or after a doctor’s visit.

Complete payment through DrSays before visiting the lab to receive a discounted rate. A $9.99 collection fee is required per order. No additional fees are charged at the lab center.

Why test?

The Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) assay quantifies the percentage of glycated hemoglobin within circulating erythrocytes, providing a longitudinal assessment of average blood glucose levels over the preceding two to three months. This pivotal metabolic metric is indispensable for diagnosing diabetes mellitus, identifying prediabetes, and evaluating the long-term efficacy of glycemic control protocols. Elevated HbA1c values strongly correlate with an increased risk of microvascular and macrovascular complications, including diabetic retinopathy, nephropathy, and cardiovascular disease. Routine monitoring facilitates targeted adjustments to insulin regimens and oral hypoglycemic therapies to optimize metabolic homeostasis.

Know more about DrSays Lab service

  • Does DrSays provide interpretation or medical management for lab results?

    DrSays provides access to laboratory testing services but does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, ongoing clinical oversight, or follow-up medical management related to Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) laboratory testing services.

    Laboratory testing is intended for informational and educational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

    Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) laboratory testing through DrSays does not establish a physician-patient relationship for purposes of ongoing medical management.

    All laboratory results should be reviewed with a qualified healthcare provider, who is responsible for clinical interpretation, diagnosis, and determining any necessary follow-up care, treatment, or additional testing.

    If you would like to discuss your lab results with a DrSays physician, you may schedule a consultation appointment separately.

  • Can I order genetic testing directly through DrSays?

    Genetic testing options through DrSays are limited, as certain state laws may require informed consent, physician involvement, genetic counseling, or additional regulatory compliance before testing can be performed.

    Genetic testing may involve complex medical information, including hereditary disease risk, carrier status, or medication response, which may require professional interpretation and follow-up care.

    Genetic testing services offered through DrSays are for informational and educational purposes only and do not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or genetic counseling. Patients are strongly encouraged to review all results with a qualified healthcare provider or licensed genetic counselor.

  • If the test I am looking for is not offered on DrSays, what should I do?

    If you cannot find the lab test you are looking for on our website, please follow these steps:

    1. Visit the LabCorp test menu to locate the appropriate test code.
    2. Contact us at support@drsays.org or send us a support message.
    3. Be sure to include the Labcorp test code and the name of the test you are requesting in your message.
  • How to access my lab results?

    You will receive an email notification once your lab results are available. To view your results, log in to your DrSays account, go to Labs, and click “Lab Results – Download”. Please ensure pop-ups are enabled.

    Please note: If your lab tests were ordered by one of our doctors, the results will be reviewed by the doctor first and may include provider comments before being released to you.

  • If I want to discuss my test results with a doctor, do I need to book an appointment?

    Yes. If you would like to review or discuss your results with a doctor, you will need to schedule a consultation appointment.
