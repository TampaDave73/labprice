    What's this test for?

    This test is ideal if you're experiencing low energy, decreased muscle mass, reduced sex drive, mood changes, or difficulty concentrating. It helps identify whether your testosterone levels might be contributing to these symptoms by measuring both total and free testosterone using highly accurate LC/MS/MS technology. Many men use this test when they suspect hormonal imbalance is affecting their vitality, performance, or overall well-being.

    How should I prepare for this test?

    If you're using a testosterone cream, avoid applying it to the inside of your elbow area for at least 24 hours before your blood draw, as this can artificially elevate your results. No fasting is required for this test. Results typically take about 7 business days, though confirmation testing may extend this timeframe if needed.

    What's included in this test?

    Testosterone, Free Testosterone, Total, MS

    This test measures both Free Testosterone and Total Testosterone to give you a complete picture of your hormone status. Free testosterone is the active form available for your body to use, while total testosterone represents all testosterone in your blood. Together, these measurements help identify whether low energy, reduced muscle mass, or other symptoms are hormone-related and can reveal issues that might be missed by testing total testosterone alone.

    What happens after I get my results?
    You'll receive detailed doctor notes in simple language explaining what your testosterone levels mean for your energy, mood, muscle mass, and overall health. These notes include personalized recommendations like lifestyle changes, supplement suggestions, or whether you should consider hormone therapy or follow up with an endocrinologist. You'll also get the standard lab report and can track your testosterone levels over time using our trends feature to see how your interventions are working.
    When will I get my results?
    You can expect your results in approximately 7 business days after your blood is drawn. If confirmation testing is required for any reason, the turnaround time may be extended slightly to ensure accuracy. You'll receive an email notification as soon as your results are ready to view in your secure online account.
    Who should consider getting this test?
    This test is important for men experiencing symptoms of low testosterone like fatigue, reduced sex drive, difficulty building muscle, or mood changes. It's also valuable for men with obesity, thyroid disorders, or liver conditions where testosterone binding proteins may be altered. Women experiencing excessive hair growth, acne, or other signs of masculinization can also use this test to evaluate hormone balance.
    What symptoms indicate I may need this test?
    Common symptoms include persistent fatigue even with adequate sleep, decreased motivation or drive, reduced muscle mass despite exercise, increased body fat especially around the midsection, low libido, difficulty concentrating, and mood changes like irritability or feeling down. Men may also notice reduced strength, less morning erections, or changes in sleep quality.
    How can I naturally support healthy testosterone levels?
    Focus on resistance training and heavy compound exercises like squats and deadlifts, which naturally boost testosterone production. Prioritize 7-9 hours of quality sleep, as testosterone is primarily produced during deep sleep. Include zinc and magnesium-rich foods like oysters, beef, pumpkin seeds, and spinach, maintain a healthy body weight, manage stress through meditation or yoga, and limit alcohol consumption.
    What is testosterone and why does it matter?
    Testosterone is the primary male sex hormone responsible for maintaining muscle mass, bone density, sex drive, energy levels, and overall vitality. It also plays important roles in mood regulation, cognitive function, and metabolism. While testosterone naturally declines with age, abnormally low levels can significantly impact quality of life, while high levels may indicate other health concerns that need attention.
    What does low testosterone mean?
    Low testosterone, sometimes called Low T, means your body isn't producing enough of this crucial hormone. This can result from natural aging, testicular problems, pituitary gland issues, obesity, certain medications, or chronic health conditions. Low levels can cause fatigue, reduced sex drive, loss of muscle mass, increased body fat, mood changes, and decreased bone density over time.
